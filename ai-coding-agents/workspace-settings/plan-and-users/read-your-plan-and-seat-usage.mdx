---
title: "Read your plan and seat usage"
---

This is where you spend most of your administration time. Everything on the page is scoped to one coding agent at a time.

The card at the top of the page summarises where you stand.

| What you see | What it means |
|:-------------|:--------------|
| **Plan name** | Your current subscription. On a trial this reads Trial Plan and is capped at a fixed number of seats. |
| **Seats used** | How many of your seats are taken. A seat is consumed when a member is active not the outstanding invitation |
| **Registered and pending** | The split beneath the bar separates members who have completed setup from invitations still awaiting a response. |
| **Upgrade plan** | Opens the upgrade path when you need more seats or Enterprise capabilities. |

<Tip>
  If the seat count is not capped yet then check the Invited list. Unanswered invitations. Delete the ones that are not going to be install or resend the invitations. Make use of all the seats within the trial period.
</Tip>
