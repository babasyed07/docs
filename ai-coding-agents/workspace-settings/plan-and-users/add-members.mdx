---
title: "Add members"
---

## Individual plan

1. **Enter** one or more work email addresses into the Add members field. Each becomes a chip, so you can invite several people at once.
2. **Click** Send invite.

Each person receives an email containing their plugin configuration instructions. They appear under Invited until they complete setup, then move to Active.

Use Resend beside any invited member who has not responded. Use the delete icon to withdraw an invitation.

## Teams plan

1. **Enter** the work email addresses into the Add members field.
2. **Select** Add members.

Members appear under Active straight away. There is no invitation and no Invited list, because you are installing the plugin on their behalf rather than asking them to do it.

<Warning>
  Adding a member on the Teams plan does not put the plugin on their machine. Until you complete Configure plugin, those members are listed as active but their sessions are not yet governed.
</Warning>
