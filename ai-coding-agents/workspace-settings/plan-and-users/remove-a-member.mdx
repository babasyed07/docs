---
title: "Remove a member"
---

Removing a member revokes their workspace access and stops them using the Reva plugin.

1. **Select** the delete icon beside the member, under Active or Invited.
2. **Read** the confirmation dialog, which names the member being removed.
3. **Select** Remove access. A confirmation message appears once the member is removed.

The seat is released and can be reassigned. The member’s past activity stays in your records, removing someone ends their access, it does not erase what was already decided and logged.

<Note>
  Removal takes effect on the member’s next session. If someone is leaving under difficult circumstances, coordinate this with the account revocation you would normally perform in your identity provider rather than relying on Reva alone.
</Note>
