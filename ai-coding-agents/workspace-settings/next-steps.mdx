title: "Next steps"
---

- **Confirm the rollout landed:** Adding a member is not the same as governing them. Track who has actually produced activity, and follow up with anyone who has not.
- **Work one agent at a time:** Complete one coding agent end to end before starting the next. Partially configured agents are easy to forget and hard to spot later.
- **Keep the member list current:** Remove people who have left and withdraw invitations who did not installed due to any circumstances. It keeps your seat count honest and your access list accurate.
- **Treat the token as a managed secret:** Rotate the workspace token on the schedule your security policy requires.
