- **Agree what you cannot tolerate:** Work through the "Risk exposure without Guardrail" rows above with whoever owns risk in your organization. That conversation is faster with concrete scenarios than with a feature list.
- **Prepare the inputs:** Populate approved machine lists, define your sensitive file categories, and confirm your approved connectors before enforcing the guardrails that depend on them.
- **Start in Monitor:** A week of Monitor data tells you which guardrails are ready to enforce and which need tuning. Enforce from evidence, not from the default.
- **Know where the detail goes:** The decision record is where the reason lives, since the agent is never told. Make sure the people who will be asked "why was this blocked" know where to look.
