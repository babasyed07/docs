## Sign in for the first time

Sign in using the access details from your workspace email. You will be asked to set your own password.

## Create your workspace

Your workspace is where your organization governs its coding agents: its policies, its team, its activity history. You provide four things.

| Field | Guidance |
|:------|:---------|
| **Workspace name** | Use your organization or business-unit name - the name developers will recognise when asked to join. Avoid environment names such as "Test" or "POC"; this is the workspace your organization keeps. |
| **Description** | Optional. A short line describing scope, useful when several teams share the workspace. You can change it later. |
| **Workspace administrator** | Confirm who administers this workspace. Defaults to you. The administrator manages policies, team members, and plugin access. |
| **Coding agents** | Select every coding agent you want Reva to govern. This is a multiple selection - choose all that apply. |

## Selecting your coding agents

Some agents can be selected immediately; others are enabled on request. If an agent your team uses is not listed, request it from the same page. Reva Expert will reach out to you.

This selection scopes the rest of setup. Each agent you choose is configured separately, because licensing, team membership, and plugin installation differ from one agent to another. You can add more agents later from Settings.

## Workspace Creation

Once you click on Create, Reva provisions your workspace. This usually completes within a few minutes while you wait.

Occasionally it takes longer. If provisioning runs past the expected time, you will see a notice that it may take up to fifteen minutes, and you will receive an email as soon as the workspace is ready. It is safe to close the browser at that point and you can return through the link in that email.
