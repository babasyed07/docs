---
title: "Onboarding at a glance"
---

Onboarding runs in four stages. The first three happen once. The fourth is repeated for each coding agent you selected.

| Stage | What you do | Typical time |
|:------|:------------|:-------------|
| **Introduction** | Sign in for the first time and review what Reva does. | 2 minutes |
| **Create workspace** | Name your workspace and select the coding agents to govern. | 1 minutes |
| **Provisioning** | Reva creates your workspace. Nothing is required from you. | Usually under 2 minutes |
| **Agent setup** | Per agent: choose a plan, add your team, install the plugin. | 10 minutes per agent |
