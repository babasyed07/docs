AI Workload Security is evolving quickly. This matrix shows where each capability stands so you can plan with confidence. Capabilities are grouped into three maturity tiers.

## Maturity Tiers

| Tier | What it means |
|:-----|:--------------|
| **Available** | Generally available in the product today. Ready for production use. |
| **Beta** | Early access — usable now, but behavior and APIs may change. Contact your Reva representative to enable. |
| **Roadmap** | Planned. Not yet available. Listed to show direction. |

## Available

| Capability | What it gives you |
|:-----------|:------------------|
| **User Delegation (On-Behalf-Of)** | Agents act strictly on behalf of the signed-in user, with the delegated identity enforced at the first hop. |
| **Token Scope Reduction** | The Reva Trust Gateway issues task-scoped transaction tokens — least scope for the task, not the user's full entitlements. |
| **Conditional Access Enforcement** | Conditional policies are evaluated at token issuance, per hop, and are visible in the decision logs. |
| **Least-Privilege Execution** | An agent's runtime actions are limited strictly to what policy permits, including spawned downstream agents with reduced scope. |
| **Tool Authorization Enforcement** | An agent can invoke only the tools it is explicitly authorized to use. |
| **End-to-End Audit Traceability** | Decision logs with trace IDs give end-to-end authorization traceability across request → policy → token hops. |

## Beta

| Capability | What it gives you |
|:-----------|:------------------|
| **Agent Inventory & Ownership** | An inventory of agents and associated identities, populated from uploaded metadata and topology. |
| **Agent Registration** | Register agents and import their metadata through the metadata-template upload. |
| **Autonomous Agent (NHI) Identity** | Agent-to-agent policies for service and non-human identities operating without an active human session. |
| **Cross-Service / Multi-API Authorization** | Each downstream service independently validates an agent's permissions (nearest-neighbour authorization). |
| **Token & Policy Propagation** | Identity tokens and authorization policies propagate through gateways and toolchains. |
| **Continuous Monitoring & Logging** | Authorization-layer monitoring of agent activity through decision logs. |

## Roadmap

| Capability | What it will give you |
|:-----------|:----------------------|
| **AI Agent Discovery** | Automatic discovery of AI agents and non-human identities across cloud, SaaS, on-prem, and AI platforms. |
| **Tool Discovery Filtering** | Agents discover or "see" only the tools and actions they're authorized to use. |
| **Kill Switch (Agent / Session)** | Immediately stop an agent or revoke its access in response to risk or a policy violation. |
| **Session Termination** | Terminate agent execution based on policy, risk signals, or manual intervention. |
| **Agent Access Certification** | Periodic or risk-based review and attestation of agent access and associated identities. |
| **Agent Decommissioning & Ownership Transfer** | Securely decommission agents and transfer ownership as responsibilities change. |

<Note>
  Roadmap items are shown to indicate direction and are not a commitment to deliver by any particular date. For the latest status or to join a Beta, contact your Reva representative.
</Note>
