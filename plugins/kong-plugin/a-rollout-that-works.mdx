---
title: "A Rollout That Works"
description: "A four-step rollout from observe-only to full enforcement, plus a production example configuration."
---

<Steps>
  <Step title="Observe">
    Attach the policy with `monitor_mode: true`. Nothing is blocked; the log and the decision log show what *would* have been refused.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Tune policy">
    Work through the would-be denials in the decision logs until the verdicts match your intent.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enforce">
    Set `monitor_mode: false` and leave `fail_open: false`.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Harden">
    Authenticate callers in front of the policy, and move `auth_token` into a vault reference.
  </Step>
</Steps>

```json Production example
{
  "reva_host": "api.example.reva.ai",
  "auth_token": "{vault://env/reva-ai-runtime-authorization-token}",
  "ssl_verify": true,
  "monitor_mode": false,
  "fail_open": false,
  "deny_status": 403,
  "deny_message": "Blocked by Reva",
  "jwt_user_claim": "sub",
  "jwt_groups_claim": "groups",
  "prompt_key": "content",
  "session_key": "X-Reva-Session-Id",
  "hop_ttl": 3600,
  "forward_traceparent": true,
  "debug": false
}
```
