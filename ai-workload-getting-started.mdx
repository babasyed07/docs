## Three Ways to Bring Agents into Reva

Before you start, know your options for getting agents, tools, models, and MCP servers into Reva:

| Method | What it is | Where |
|:-------|:-----------|:------|
| **1. Upload a CSV template** | Download the metadata template, fill it, upload, and review. Best for manual registration. | [Upload from Template](/ai-workload-schema-upload-template) |
| **2. Ingest via the API** | Push entities programmatically with the Data Fabric entity API, using your **Store ID** + **PDP Auth Token**. | [Developer Settings](/ai-workload-developer-settings) → [Create Entities API](/api-reference/entity-ingestion/create-entities) |
| **3. Discovery integrations** *(Roadmap)* | Azure and other discovery sources auto-add agents to your repository; you then **Add from Repository**. | [Azure Agent Integration](/azure-agent-integration) |

<Note>
  Already have agents in a connected repository? Use [Add from Repository](/ai-workload-schema-add-from-repository) when you create the policy store.
</Note>

## Your First Run

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open AI Insights">
    On the Home page, switch to the **AI** view. On day one, the **Agentic Workload** panel is empty.

    <Frame caption="The empty Agentic Workload panel on the AI Insights home.">
      <img src="images/ai-workload-home-empty.png" alt="Empty Agentic Workload panel with Add Agentic Workloads button" />
    </Frame>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add an agentic workload">
    Click **Add Agentic Workloads** to create your first agentic policy store. (You can also start from **Manage Policies → + → Agent**.) See [Create an Agentic Policy Store](/ai-workload-create-policy-store).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Onboard your agents">
    Upload the metadata template or add agents from a connected repository, then **review** the parsed entities before proceeding.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Land on the topology">
    Reva renders your agentic **topology** — the hub where you assign policies. Guardrails are auto-applied to the new store.

    <Frame caption="A populated agentic workload inventory.">
      <img src="images/ai-workload-home-filled.png" alt="Populated agentic workload inventory" />
    </Frame>
  </Step>
</Steps>

Ready? Start with [Create an Agentic Policy Store](/ai-workload-create-policy-store).
