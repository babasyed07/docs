## The Inventory and Your Policy Stores

Every Reva tenant is bootstrapped with a global agentic policy store called **`GlobalAgentWorkloadApp`**. Every entity you ingest — agents, MCP servers, tools, models, and skills — lands here and populates your tenant-wide **inventory** (the **Agentic Workload** panel on the AI home).

<Frame caption="GlobalAgentWorkloadApp — the global agentic store every tenant starts with — listed alongside your own policy stores.">
  <img src="images/ai-workload-manage-policies-list.png" alt="Manage Policies list showing GlobalAgentWorkloadApp" />
</Frame>

You generally **don't author policies directly in `GlobalAgentWorkloadApp`** — it's the shared landing zone for everything you ingest. (You *can*, but it isn't the intended workflow.) Instead, create one or more **purpose-specific policy stores** — for example *Secure Banking – Agents* or *SupplyChain Application* — and add the relevant entities from your inventory into each. Every store keeps its own schema, policies, and decision logs.

From the inventory, click **Manage** on any entity to work on its access. Because an entity can belong to several stores, Reva asks **which policy store** you want to open, then takes you to that store's topology.

<Frame caption="Manage an entity: pick which policy store to open.">
  <img src="images/ai-workload-manage-entity-select-store.png" alt="Manage Entity dialog with a Select Policy Store dropdown" />
</Frame>

## Three Ways to Fill Your Inventory

Bring agents, tools, models, and MCP servers into your inventory in any of three ways:

| Method | What it is | Where |
|:-------|:-----------|:------|
| **1. Upload a CSV template** | Download the metadata template, fill it, upload, and review. Best for manual registration. | [Upload from Template](/ai-workload-schema-upload-template) |
| **2. Ingest via the API** | Push entities programmatically with the Data Fabric entity API, using the **`GlobalAgentWorkloadApp`** **Store ID** + **PDP Auth Token**. | [Developer Settings](/ai-workload-developer-settings) → [Create Entities API](/api-reference/entity-ingestion/create-entities) |
| **3. Discovery integrations** *(Roadmap)* | Azure and other discovery sources auto-add agents to your inventory; you then **Add from Repository**. | [Azure Agent Integration](/azure-agent-integration) |

<Note>
  Already have agents in your inventory? Use [Add from Repository](/ai-workload-schema-add-from-repository) when you create the policy store to pull them in.
</Note>

## Your First Run

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open AI Insights">
    On the Home page, switch to the **AI** view. On day one, the **Agentic Workload** panel is empty.

    <Frame caption="The empty Agentic Workload panel on the AI Insights home.">
      <img src="images/ai-workload-home-empty.png" alt="Empty Agentic Workload panel with Add Agentic Workloads button" />
    </Frame>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add an agentic workload">
    Click **Add Agentic Workloads** to create your first agentic policy store. (You can also start from **Manage Policies → + → Agent**.) See [Create an Agentic Policy Store](/ai-workload-create-policy-store).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Onboard your agents">
    Upload the metadata template or add agents from your inventory, then **review** the parsed entities before proceeding.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Land on the topology">
    Reva renders your agentic **topology** — the hub where you assign policies. Guardrails are auto-applied to the new store.

    <Frame caption="A populated agentic workload inventory.">
      <img src="images/ai-workload-home-filled.png" alt="Populated agentic workload inventory" />
    </Frame>
  </Step>
</Steps>
