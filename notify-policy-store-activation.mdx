---
title: "Notification: Notify on Policy Store Activation"
---
When an application is onboarded in Reva and its associated policy store is successfully activated, users receive a real-time notification confirming the activation. This ensures that all relevant team members are immediately informed when the policy store becomes operational. 

## Activation Notification 

The notification is triggered after the policy store activation process completes during application onboarding. 

Details included in the notification: 
- Policy Store Name 
- Application Name 
- Owner’s Username 
- Activation Timestamp 

These activation notifications provide complete traceability, helping teams track policy onboarding and activation events, ensuring proper governance, auditability, and compliance across environments.
