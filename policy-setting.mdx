---
title: "Policy Settings"
---
The Policy Settings Tab is the central workspace for configuring ownership, environment details, data sources, and policy publishing destinations in Reva. It allows administrators and developers to control how policies are managed, tested, and deployed across environments. From here, you can set who owns or collaborates on a policy store, decide which dataset powers simulations and analytics, and choose the policy engine type (AVP, Cedar, OPA) for publishing.  

## Info & Ownership
Manage the identity, description, and permissions for the policy store.  
1. **Environment Name**  
   A unique label for the environment where the policy store runs.  
   Example: dev, qa, or production.
2. **Description**  
   A free-text field describing the application or system linked to this policy store.
   Example:  
   “Comprehensive banking platform for customer account management and internal staff operations with advanced security controls.”
3. **Owners**  
   Users with full control over the policy store. Owners can create, update, and delete policies and configuration.
   Example: appadmin.
4. **Collaborators**  
   Define who can view or edit the policy store:  
   **Viewers:** Read-only access, suitable for auditors or compliance reviewers.  
   Example: auditor-user  
   **Editors:** Can update configurations and policies but not delete the store.  
   Example: developer1.
## Policy Simulation Data configuration
Configure the preferred dataset for policy evaluation, simulations, and analytics.
1. Preferred Data Source
   Select the data environment for running policies:
   - **Simulation:** Test policies against simulated datasets before going live.  
     Example: Use “Simulation” while drafting rules to check impact without affecting production.
   - **Production:** Apply live datasets for real policy enforcement and monitoring.  
     Example: Use “Production” when finalizing authorization checks for SecureBank transactions.  
## Policy Publish
Set destinations and integrations for publishing and running policies.
1. **Policy Type Selection** (when creating new store)  
   Choose the engine or framework for policy enforcement:  
   - **AVP** (Amazon Verified Permissions)
   - **Cedar** (Cedar policy language)
   - **OPA** (Open Policy Agent).
<Info>Example: Select “AVP” if integrating with Amazon Verified Permissions) to manage the policy store.</Info>
2. **Connection Settings** (when publishing with AVP)
   - **Connection Name:** A user-friendly name for the publishing connection.  
     Example: east-2.
   - **Connection Type:** Indicates the enforcement engine, e.g., AVP.
   - **External ID:** Unique identifier for cross-account trust and API calls.  
     Example: LD4qdTVkc6ZAmExtVJxHnG.
   - **Account ID:** AWS account or system identifier for integration.  
     Example: 123456789012.
   - **Region:** Region where the policy engine is deployed.  
     Example: us-east-2.
3. **History Button**  
   Allows you to view the publish history of policies, including timestamps and status.
