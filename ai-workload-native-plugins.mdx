  **Coming soon.** Native plugins are under development. This page previews what they'll cover — contact your Reva representative for early access.

For agents you can't instrument with code, Reva is building **native plugins** that enforce authorization through each platform's own extension model — no SDK or custom HTTP steps required. Every action is still evaluated by the [Reva Trust Guardian](/ai-workload-security-overview).

Planned coverage:

- **No-code / SaaS agents** — Salesforce Agentforce and similar platforms.
- **Coding agents** — Claude Code, Cursor, and Kiro, securing their access to enterprise systems.
