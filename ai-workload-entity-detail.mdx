---
title: "View & Edit Entity Details"
description: "Review and edit an entity's attributes, relationships, skills, and metadata."
---

Each entity in your topology has a detail view where you review and edit its attributes, relationships, skills, and metadata. Accurate metadata makes your policies precise — many conditions test values like `riskTier` or an agent's skills.

## Review an Entity

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open an entity">
    In the topology (or while onboarding), select an entity to open its detail view. For an agent, this shows the **Agent Details** — name, id, data classification, risk tier, max chain depth, status, path URL, and more.

    <Frame caption="An agent's detail view.">
      <img src="images/ai-workload-onboard-metadata.png" alt="Agent details view" />
    </Frame>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Edit the metadata">
    Click **Edit** to change any field, then **Save**. Updated attributes are immediately available to policies that reference them.

    <Frame caption="Edit an entity's metadata.">
      <img src="images/ai-workload-entity-edit.png" alt="Edit entity metadata" />
    </Frame>
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Manage Skills

An agent's **skills** describe what it can do — and you can write [intent/skill-based policies](/ai-workload-policy-design) against them. While onboarding, or any time after, you can **add, edit, or delete** an agent's skills.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the agent's Skills">
    In the agent's detail view, find the **Skills** section.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add or edit a skill">
    Click **Add Skill** (or edit an existing one) and set its **Name**, **ID**, **Description**, **Source**, and **Risk Tier**.

    <Frame caption="Add a skill to an agent.">
      <img src="images/ai-workload-add-skills.png" alt="Add a skill dialog" />
    </Frame>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save">
    Save. The skill is now available for intent/skill-based policies.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Tip>
  Set `riskTier` and `dataClassification` thoughtfully — they're commonly used in on-behalf-of and context-based policy conditions.
</Tip>
